# Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC) (saic)

Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC) is a Reston, Virginia based technology integrator that delivers mission IT, enterprise IT, engineering services, and professional services to U.S. federal civilian, defense, intelligence community, and space customers. With approximately 24,000 employees and roughly $7.3 billion in annual revenue, SAIC focuses on direct technical support and integration for government organizations, spanning CJADC2, data and AI, digital transformation, quantum technologies, cloud, cybersecurity, digital workplace, digital engineering, and systems integration. SAIC has expanded its capabilities through acquisitions including Engility (2018), Unisys Federal (2020), Halfaker (2021, DoD healthcare), Koverse (2021, secure data lake for classified environments), and Forfeiture Support Associates (2023). No public REST APIs, developer portal, OpenAPI specifications, SDKs, or open source GitHub organization have been identified for SAIC — its software and platforms are delivered under federal contract to government customers rather than as externally-consumable developer products.
